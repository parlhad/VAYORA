=============================================

PURPOSE
-------
This knowledge base provides general environmental, air-quality,
weather-impact, and health-protection knowledge for VAYORA.

IMPORTANT:
- Live AQI values must come from the live AQI service.
- Live weather values must come from the live weather service.
- This knowledge base must NOT be used to invent current environmental values.

== AQI BASICS ==
----------------

AQI stands for Air Quality Index. It is a numerical indicator used to
communicate how polluted the air is and the potential health concern
associated with the measured air quality.

VAYORA's live AQI value must always come from its configured live AQI
source rather than from this knowledge base.

General AQI interpretation used by VAYORA:

AQI 0-50:
Good — Air quality is generally satisfactory and poses little or no
health risk under the applicable AQI scale.

AQI 51-100:
Moderate — Air quality is generally acceptable, although unusually
sensitive individuals may experience effects.

AQI 101-150:
Unhealthy for Sensitive Groups — Sensitive groups may experience
health effects, while the general population is less likely to be
affected.

AQI 151-200:
Unhealthy — Some members of the general population may experience
health effects, with greater concern for sensitive groups.

AQI 201-300:
Very Unhealthy — Health alert conditions; the risk of health effects
is increased for everyone.

AQI 301+:
Hazardous — Emergency-level air-quality conditions with potentially
serious health effects.

IMPORTANT:
AQI scales can differ between countries and systems. VAYORA should
use the category produced by its deterministic health-interpretation
logic for live responses.


== KEY POLLUTANTS ==
--------------------

PM2.5:
Fine particulate matter with an aerodynamic diameter of about
2.5 micrometres or smaller. These particles can penetrate deeply
into the respiratory system and are strongly associated with
cardiovascular and respiratory health effects.

PM10:
Particulate matter with an aerodynamic diameter of about 10 micrometres
or smaller. It includes larger particles such as dust and can affect
the respiratory system.

NO2:
Nitrogen dioxide. It is commonly associated with combustion processes,
especially traffic and fuel burning. Exposure can irritate the
respiratory system and is associated with respiratory health effects.

SO2:
Sulfur dioxide. It is commonly produced by burning sulfur-containing
fuels and can irritate the respiratory system. People with asthma
may be particularly sensitive.

O3:
Ground-level ozone. It is a secondary pollutant formed through
chemical reactions involving precursor pollutants in sunlight.
It can irritate the airways and contribute to coughing and breathing
difficulty.

CO:
Carbon monoxide. A poisonous gas produced by incomplete combustion.
It reduces the blood's ability to carry oxygen and can be dangerous
at sufficiently high exposure levels.

IMPORTANT:
Pollutant measurements returned by WAQI must be interpreted according
to the units and meaning provided by the source. Do not assume that
every WAQI IAQI value is a concentration in µg/m³.


== HEALTH EFFECTS OF AIR POLLUTION ==
-------------------------------------

Short-term exposure can be associated with:
- Eye, nose, and throat irritation
- Coughing
- Wheezing
- Shortness of breath
- Breathing discomfort
- Worsening of asthma symptoms
- Other respiratory irritation

Long-term or repeated exposure to air pollution is associated with
increased risk of:
- Cardiovascular disease
- Stroke
- Chronic respiratory disease
- Lung cancer
- Respiratory infections
- Other adverse health outcomes

Fine particulate pollution is especially important because very small
particles can penetrate deeply into the respiratory system and are
associated with cardiovascular and respiratory disease.

WHO estimates that the combined effects of ambient and household air
pollution are associated with approximately 7 million premature deaths
globally each year.

VAYORA should present this as population-level evidence, not as a
prediction of an individual's health outcome.


== VULNERABLE / SENSITIVE GROUPS ==
-----------------------------------

Children:
Developing lungs and higher ventilation relative to body size can
increase concern during significant pollution exposure.

Older adults:
Older adults may be more vulnerable to the effects of air pollution,
particularly when underlying health conditions are present.

Pregnant people:
Air pollution exposure is associated with adverse pregnancy and
birth outcomes. Avoiding unnecessary exposure is appropriate when
air quality is poor.

People with asthma or other respiratory disease:
Poor air quality can worsen respiratory symptoms and may increase
the risk of exacerbations.

People with cardiovascular disease:
Air pollution, particularly particulate pollution, is associated
with cardiovascular health risks.

IMPORTANT:
VAYORA must not diagnose a condition or tell a person to change
prescribed medication.


== PERSONAL EXPOSURE REDUCTION ==
--------------------------------

When air quality is poor:

- Reduce unnecessary outdoor exposure.
- Consider postponing strenuous outdoor exercise.
- Keep indoor spaces appropriately protected from outdoor pollution.
- Follow local public-health guidance when available.
- If outdoor exposure cannot be avoided, use a properly fitted
  particulate respirator such as an appropriate N95 when suitable.
- Avoid adding indoor pollution through smoking, burning materials,
  incense, or other combustion sources.
- Monitor live AQI conditions when planning outdoor activities.

The appropriate action depends on the actual AQI, pollutant mix,
weather, activity level, and individual sensitivity.

VAYORA should not claim that one fixed AQI threshold automatically
applies to every person or activity.


== MASKS ==
----------

A properly fitted particulate respirator such as an N95 can reduce
inhalation of airborne particles when used correctly.

Important limitations:
- Particle respirators do not provide protection from every pollutant.
- They do not remove carbon monoxide.
- Fit and correct use are important.
- Facial hair or poor fit can reduce effectiveness.
- Users should follow applicable public-health and occupational
  guidance.

VAYORA should recommend a suitable particulate respirator when
appropriate rather than presenting a mask as complete protection
from air pollution.


== INDOOR AIR QUALITY ==
------------------------

Useful exposure-reduction measures include:

- Keep windows and doors closed when outdoor pollution is high,
  when appropriate for the building and ventilation situation.
- Use an appropriately sized portable air cleaner when available.
- A HEPA filter is designed to remove at least 99.97% of particles
  of 0.3 micrometres under the applicable test conditions.
- Replace or maintain filters according to manufacturer instructions.
- Avoid smoking indoors.
- Avoid unnecessary burning of incense, candles, or other materials
  that generate indoor particles.
- Use kitchen exhaust ventilation when cooking produces pollutants.
- Wet cleaning can help reduce the resuspension of settled dust.
- Maintain adequate ventilation when outdoor air quality permits.

IMPORTANT:
Air cleaners designed primarily for particles do not necessarily
remove gases such as carbon monoxide.


== INDOOR PLANTS ==
-------------------

Indoor plants may have aesthetic and psychological benefits, but
ordinary household plants should NOT be presented by VAYORA as a
reliable replacement for ventilation, source control, filtration,
or an air purifier.

VAYORA should prioritize:
1. Removing or reducing pollution sources.
2. Appropriate ventilation when outdoor conditions permit.
3. Effective filtration when needed.


== OUTDOOR ACTIVITY ==
----------------------

Outdoor activity decisions should consider:

- Current AQI
- Pollutant levels
- Weather conditions
- Duration of exposure
- Exercise intensity
- Individual sensitivity
- Whether the activity can be moved indoors

When air pollution is high:
- Reduce strenuous outdoor exercise.
- Prefer shorter exposure when outdoor activity is necessary.
- Sensitive individuals should take additional precautions.
- Follow the live AQI category and VAYORA's deterministic health
  guidance rather than relying on one universal threshold.

For exercise-related questions, VAYORA should explain the decision
using current live environmental data whenever available.


== WEATHER AND AIR POLLUTION ==
-------------------------------

Wind:
Higher wind speeds can help disperse pollutants in some situations,
while stagnant or low-wind conditions can allow pollutants to
accumulate.

Temperature inversion:
An inversion can limit vertical mixing and trap pollutants close
to the ground.

Rain:
Rain can remove some particles from the atmosphere and may temporarily
improve particulate air quality. However, rainfall does not guarantee
clean air and its effect depends on rainfall intensity, duration,
wind, pollutant sources, and local conditions.

Humidity:
High humidity can increase haze and can affect particulate matter
through interactions with water in the atmosphere. High humidity does
not automatically mean that pollution is higher.

Sunlight:
Sunlight drives photochemical reactions that can contribute to the
formation of ground-level ozone when precursor pollutants are present.

Weather should therefore be used as context for environmental
interpretation rather than as proof of a specific AQI value.


== CURRENT WEATHER INFORMATION ==
---------------------------------

When the user asks about current weather, VAYORA should use the
live OpenWeather service whenever available.

Useful current weather fields may include:
- Temperature
- Feels-like temperature
- Humidity
- Wind speed
- Wind direction
- Cloudiness
- Visibility
- Atmospheric pressure
- Rain during the available observation period
- Snow when applicable
- Current weather condition

IMPORTANT:
If rain data is unavailable, VAYORA must say that precipitation
was not reported by the current weather source.

VAYORA must NOT claim:
"It is raining now"
unless the live data actually supports that statement.

Similarly, absence of reported rain does not prove that it is
definitely not raining.


== RAIN QUESTIONS ==
--------------------

When a user asks:

"Is it raining in Pune?"

VAYORA should check the live weather data.

Possible evidence includes:
- Current weather condition
- Reported precipitation amount
- Rainfall reported by the source
- Weather forecast when the user asks about future rain

If precipitation data is unavailable:
- State that precipitation was not reported.
- Do not convert missing data into "no rain."

When the user asks:

"Will it rain tomorrow?"

VAYORA should use forecast data rather than current conditions.


== HEAT AND TEMPERATURE QUESTIONS ==
------------------------------------

When a user asks about heat:

VAYORA should distinguish between:
- Current temperature
- Feels-like temperature
- Humidity
- Wind
- Forecast temperature
- Heat-related risk when sufficient information is available

High temperature combined with high humidity can increase heat stress
because evaporation of sweat becomes less effective.

For heat-related questions, VAYORA should avoid making a medical
diagnosis.

If a user reports severe heat-related symptoms such as confusion,
fainting, or severe weakness, VAYORA should recommend urgent medical
attention rather than treating the situation as ordinary discomfort.


== WEATHER + AQI COMBINED INTERPRETATION ==
--------------------------------------------

Weather and air quality can interact.

Examples:

Low wind + high pollution:
Pollutants may remain concentrated near the ground.

Rain + particulate pollution:
Rain may temporarily reduce some airborne particles.

High humidity + pollution:
Haze and visibility problems may become more noticeable.

Strong sunlight + precursor pollutants:
Ground-level ozone formation can increase under suitable conditions.

Important:
These are environmental relationships, not guarantees.

VAYORA should always prioritize measured live data over general
weather assumptions.


== HEALTH TIPS ==
----------------

General exposure-reduction practices:

- Reduce exposure during poor air-quality periods.
- Avoid strenuous outdoor activity during high pollution.
- Use appropriate particulate protection when necessary.
- Keep indoor pollution sources low.
- Use effective filtration when appropriate.
- Follow prescribed treatment plans from healthcare professionals.
- Monitor symptoms and seek medical advice when symptoms are
  concerning or persistent.


== HOME REMEDIES AND COMFORT MEASURES ==
----------------------------------------

Home remedies should be presented only as comfort measures and NOT
as treatments that remove pollutants from the lungs or cure disease.

Possible comfort measures for minor irritation may include:
- Drinking water or warm fluids for throat comfort.
- Honey may soothe cough in appropriate individuals, but it is not
  a treatment for the underlying cause.
- Saline nasal rinsing may provide relief for some people when used
  correctly with safe water and proper hygiene.

VAYORA must NOT claim that:
- Jaggery "clears the lungs."
- Tulsi cures pollution-related damage.
- Steam inhalation removes pollutants from the lungs.
- Turmeric milk reverses pollution damage.
- Any food detoxifies the lungs.
- A home remedy can replace medical treatment.

For respiratory symptoms, the safest first step is exposure reduction.
Persistent, severe, or worsening symptoms require appropriate medical
evaluation.


== BREATHING EXERCISES ==
-------------------------

Breathing exercises may help some people with relaxation or breathing
control, but VAYORA should not claim that they remove air pollutants
from the lungs or directly increase blood oxygen in a guaranteed way.

Examples include:
- Diaphragmatic breathing
- Controlled breathing exercises
- Relaxation-focused breathing

People with significant breathing difficulty should not rely on
breathing exercises instead of appropriate medical care.


== ASTHMA / RESPIRATORY CONDITIONS ==
-------------------------------------

For people with asthma or other respiratory conditions:

- Poor air quality may worsen symptoms.
- Reduce unnecessary exposure during pollution episodes.
- Follow the person's prescribed asthma or respiratory action plan.
- Continue prescribed medication according to medical advice.
- Do not start, stop, or change medication based solely on VAYORA.

If severe breathing difficulty, inability to speak normally because
of breathlessness, bluish lips, confusion, or rapidly worsening
symptoms occur, urgent medical care may be required.


== CARDIOVASCULAR HEALTH ==
---------------------------

Air pollution, particularly particulate pollution, is associated
with cardiovascular health risks.

People with cardiovascular disease should consider reducing
unnecessary exposure during poor air-quality conditions and follow
their healthcare professional's advice.

VAYORA must not prescribe medication or provide individualized
medical treatment plans.


== CHILDREN AND OUTDOOR SPORTS ==
---------------------------------

Children may be more vulnerable to air pollution.

During poor air-quality conditions:
- Consider reducing strenuous outdoor activity.
- Prefer cleaner indoor environments when appropriate.
- Monitor the live AQI before outdoor sports.
- Follow school, sports, and local public-health guidance when
  available.

Do not use one universal AQI number as an automatic rule for every
child or every activity.


== PREGNANCY ==
--------------

Pregnant people should minimize unnecessary exposure to significant
air pollution when practical.

VAYORA should provide general exposure-reduction guidance and should
not provide individualized pregnancy treatment or medical diagnosis.


== WARNING SIGNS ==
-------------------

VAYORA should distinguish ordinary irritation from potentially
serious symptoms.

Seek appropriate medical attention for concerning symptoms such as:
- Severe or worsening shortness of breath
- Chest pain or pressure
- Fainting
- Confusion
- Severe weakness
- Bluish lips or skin
- Serious asthma symptoms not responding to the person's prescribed
  rescue plan

For emergencies, users should contact their local emergency medical
service.


== POLLUTION SOURCES ==
----------------------

Common sources include:

Transportation:
- Vehicle exhaust
- Traffic-related combustion

Industry and energy:
- Factories
- Power generation
- Fuel combustion

Construction:
- Construction dust
- Demolition activity

Road and soil dust:
- Resuspended particles from roads and exposed surfaces

Agriculture:
- Crop-residue burning
- Agricultural activities

Household sources:
- Solid-fuel combustion
- Smoking
- Burning materials indoors

Waste:
- Open waste burning
- Waste-processing emissions

Fireworks:
Can produce temporary spikes in particulate pollution and other

== POLLUTION AND CLIMATE ==
---------------------------

Air pollution and climate change are connected but are not the same
thing.

Activities such as fossil-fuel combustion can contribute both to
greenhouse-gas emissions and air pollution.

Reducing fossil-fuel combustion, improving energy efficiency, using
cleaner energy, improving transportation, and reducing open burning
can provide benefits for both air quality and climate.

Some pollutants have short atmospheric lifetimes and can have strong
climate effects, while greenhouse gases behave differently.

VAYORA should explain this distinction when users ask about the
relationship between pollution and climate change.


== ENVIRONMENTAL EFFECTS ==
---------------------------

Air pollution can affect more than human health.

Depending on the pollutant and exposure:
- Ozone can damage vegetation.
- Nitrogen and sulfur compounds can contribute to ecosystem
  acidification and nutrient imbalance.
- Particulate pollution can reduce visibility.
- Black carbon and other short-lived pollutants can influence
  climate and environmental systems.

Environmental impacts vary by pollutant, concentration, location,
weather, and duration of exposure.


== SOLUTIONS ==
--------------

Ways to reduce pollution include:

Individual and community actions:
- Reduce unnecessary vehicle use.
- Use public transport, walking, or cycling when conditions are safe.
- Avoid open burning.
- Improve energy efficiency.
- Reduce indoor combustion pollution.
- Support cleaner energy and transportation.

Urban and government actions:
- Better public transportation
- Cleaner industrial processes
- Dust-control measures
- Waste-management improvements
- Cleaner household energy
- Air-quality monitoring
- Strong emission standards
- Urban planning and green infrastructure

Electric vehicles can reduce tailpipe emissions, although their
overall environmental impact depends on electricity generation,
vehicle production, battery production, and other factors.

Government programs such as India's National Clean Air Programme
(NCAP) aim to improve air quality through coordinated pollution
reduction efforts.


== AIR QUALITY DECISION LOGIC FOR VAYORA ==
-------------------------------------------

When a user asks whether they should go outside, exercise, run,
walk, cycle, play sports, or travel:

VAYORA should prioritize:

1. Current live AQI.
2. AQI category and deterministic health interpretation.
3. Relevant pollutant information.
4. Current weather.
5. Forecast information when the user asks about future conditions.
6. User's stated activity.
7. Exposure duration and intensity.
8. Sensitive-group considerations when relevant.

VAYORA should give a practical recommendation supported by available
verified information.

Do not invent environmental values.


== LIVE DATA VS KNOWLEDGE ==
----------------------------

LIVE DATA:
- Current AQI
- Current pollutant readings
- Monitoring station
- AQI observation time
- Current temperature
- Current humidity
- Current wind
- Current precipitation information
- Current visibility
- Current weather condition
- Forecast information

These must come from VAYORA's configured live services.

KNOWLEDGE:
- What AQI means
- What pollutants mean
- General health effects
- General exposure-reduction practices
- Pollution science
- Weather-pollution relationships
- Environmental science
- General health-protection information

Gemini may explain verified live data using retrieved knowledge,
but it must not invent live measurements.


== DATA UNCERTAINTY ==
----------------------

If a live source is unavailable:

- Say that the requested live information could not be retrieved.
- Do not guess the current AQI.
- Do not guess the current temperature.
- Do not guess whether it is raining.
- Do not guess future weather.
- Clearly separate unavailable information from general knowledge.

If a live value comes from cache, VAYORA should distinguish cached
or recent information from a freshly retrieved observation when the
application exposes that status.


== MEDICAL SAFETY ==
--------------------

VAYORA is an environmental intelligence assistant, not a doctor.

VAYORA should:
- Provide general health information.
- Encourage exposure reduction.
- Explain environmental risks.
- Encourage professional care when symptoms are concerning.

VAYORA should NOT:
- Diagnose diseases.
- Prescribe medicines.
- Tell users to stop prescribed medication.
- Guarantee that a home remedy will cure a condition.
- Claim that food or plants detoxify the lungs.
- Invent medical measurements.
- Present uncertain information as certain.


== RESPONSE PRINCIPLES ==
-------------------------

When answering users:

1. Answer the actual question first.
2. Use live data when the question requires current information.
3. Use RAG knowledge for general explanation and context.
4. Clearly separate measured facts from AI interpretation.
5. Avoid unnecessary alarm.
6. Increase urgency when verified environmental risk is high.
7. State uncertainty when information is unavailable.
8. Never fabricate missing data.
9. Keep health advice practical and evidence-aware.
10. Never replace professional medical care with AI advice.


== IMPORTANT FINAL RULE ==
--------------------------

VAYORA should always prioritize:

- Verified live environmental data
- Deterministic AQI interpretation
- Evidence-based knowledge
- Exposure reduction
- Clear uncertainty
- Safe health guidance
- Practical answers to the user's actual question
